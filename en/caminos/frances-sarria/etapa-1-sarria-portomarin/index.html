Stage 1: Sarria – Portomarín

The stage where the 100 km count begins. Many people start in Sarria, so the Camino is at its busiest. Oak woods, Galician hamlets and the crossing of the Belesar reservoir on arrival at Portomarín.

Did you know?

Old Portomarín lies underwater. When the Belesar reservoir was built in 1962, the town was moved stone by stone up the hill. Its fortress-church of San Nicolás was dismantled and rebuilt numbered, like a giant puzzle!
