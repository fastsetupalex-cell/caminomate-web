Stage 21: León – San Martín del Camino

Leaving León past the San Marcos Parador, you climb to the Virgen del Camino sanctuary. Then the straight, monotonous track of the Leonese páramo carries you to San Martín del Camino.

Did you know?

The modernist Virgen del Camino sanctuary (1955-1961), by friar Francisco Coello, bears on its façade thirteen six-metre bronze figures by sculptor Subirachs: the apostles and the Virgin.
