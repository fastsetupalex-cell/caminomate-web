Stage 31: Palas de Rei – Arzúa

A long, rolling stage through rural Galicia, crossing medieval hamlets and river valleys. The classic stop is Melide, capital of Galician octopus, before finishing in Arzúa, famous for its cheese.

Did you know?

In Melide the French Way merges with the Primitive Way, the oldest pilgrim route, walked from Oviedo by King Alfonso II back in the 9th century.
