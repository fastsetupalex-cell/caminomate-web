Stage 6: Estella – Los Arcos

Just after Estella comes the celebrated wine fountain at the Irache winery. The stage passes Villamayor de Monjardín and then a long, remote stretch across open farmland to Los Arcos.

Did you know?

The Irache winery's wine fountain has poured free wine for pilgrims since 1991; a plaque invites you to drink so you reach Santiago 'with strength and vitality'.
