Stage 11: Belorado – San Juan de Ortega

A transitional stage toward the Montes de Oca, first across fields and then climbing through pine and oak woods. After Villafranca Montes de Oca a lonely forest stretch leads to San Juan de Ortega.

Did you know?

In the church of San Juan de Ortega, at the spring and autumn equinoxes, a beam of sunlight illuminates a carved Annunciation capital, the celebrated 'miracle of the light'.
