Stage 19: El Burgo Ranero – Mansilla de las Mulas

A short, flat leg across the Leonese meseta, following the tree-lined path beside the road. You pass Reliegos before reaching Mansilla de las Mulas, a walled town on the Esla river.

Did you know?

Mansilla de las Mulas still keeps much of its medieval rammed-earth and stone wall, with towers and stretches once encircling the town, today a listed historic site.
