Stage 27: O Cebreiro – Triacastela

A Galician mountain stage running along the heights of Alto de San Roque and Alto do Poio before dropping through woods and hamlets into the valley. It ends at Triacastela, a traditional day's end since medieval times.

Did you know?

At Triacastela pilgrims picked up limestone blocks to carry to the kilns of Castañeda, helping to supply lime for building Santiago's cathedral.
