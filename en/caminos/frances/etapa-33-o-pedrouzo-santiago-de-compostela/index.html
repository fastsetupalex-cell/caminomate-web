Stage 33: O Pedrouzo – Santiago de Compostela

The final stage weaves through eucalyptus to Lavacolla and up Monte do Gozo, where the cathedral towers first come into view. The descent leads the pilgrim into the Praza do Obradoiro.

Did you know?

Tradition tells that at Lavacolla pilgrims washed in the stream to reach Santiago clean; and on Monte do Gozo the first to spot the cathedral was crowned "king" of the group.
