Stage 14: Hornillos del Camino – Castrojeriz

A classic meseta stage across wide grain fields to Hontanas, hidden in a fold of land. Near Castrojeriz the ruined convent of San Antón spans the path with its Gothic arch, beneath the castle hill.

Did you know?

The San Antón monks treated pilgrims suffering 'St. Anthony's fire' (ergotism) and left bread in the niches of the arch, a tradition of charity still remembered today.
