Camino Francés · Stage 7 of 33Los Arcos → Logroño

A long day through vineyards and open countryside, first in Navarre and then in La Rioja, mixing tracks and roadside paths. You cross Sansol, Torres del Rio and Viana, still in Navarre, before reaching Logrono, the wine capital of La Rioja.

Did you know?
