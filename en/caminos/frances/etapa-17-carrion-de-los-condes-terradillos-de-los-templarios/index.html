Stage 17: Carrión de los Condes – Terradillos de los Templarios

The loneliest meseta stage: a long, straight, shadeless stretch of old Roman road to Calzadilla de la Cueza, with no villages between. It continues to Terradillos de los Templarios.

Did you know?

The long straight stretch follows the Roman Via Aquitana; its ancient paved surface gives Calzadilla de la Cueza its name, meaning 'little causeway'.
