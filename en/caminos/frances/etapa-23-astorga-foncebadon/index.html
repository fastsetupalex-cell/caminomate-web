Stage 23: Astorga – Foncebadón

You enter the Maragatería and begin climbing into the Montes de León. Past Murias, Santa Catalina and Rabanal del Camino, the ascent reaches Foncebadón, a near-abandoned mountain hamlet revived by the Camino.

Did you know?

Near the route, Castrillo de los Polvazares keeps its cobbled streets and reddish stone houses intact, one of the best-preserved Maragato villages and famous for its hearty cocido stew.
