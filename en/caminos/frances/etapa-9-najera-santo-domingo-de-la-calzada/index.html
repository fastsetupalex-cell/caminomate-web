Camino Francés · Stage 9 of 33Nájera → Santo Domingo de la Calzada

A short, easy stage across grain fields and Rioja vineyards, passing through Azofra. It ends in Santo Domingo de la Calzada, a town born to serve pilgrims and famed for the live hens in its cathedral.

Did you know?

Tradition tells of the miracle of the rooster and hen; in memory of it, two live hens are still kept inside the cathedral, recalling a pilgrim hanged unjustly who came back to life.
