Stage 1: Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port – Roncesvalles

The legendary opening stage crosses the Pyrenees on the Napoleon route from Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port, a long, demanding climb through high pastures to the Lepoeder pass, then down to the monastery at Roncesvalles.

Did you know?

Roncesvalles is where, in the year 778, Charlemagne's rearguard was ambushed; the event inspired the medieval epic poem 'The Song of Roland'.
