Stage 29: Sarria – Portomarín

The first stage from Sarria, busy with pilgrims, through sunken lanes, oak groves and Galician hamlets marked by kilometre stones. You cross the Belesar reservoir on a long bridge to reach Portomarín, rebuilt on the hilltop.

Did you know?

Old Portomarín was flooded by the Belesar reservoir in the 1960s; its fortress-church of San Nicolás was dismantled and moved stone by stone to the new town.
