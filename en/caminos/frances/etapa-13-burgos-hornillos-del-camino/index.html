Stage 13: Burgos – Hornillos del Camino

Leaving Burgos and its Gothic cathedral, the trail climbs onto the open meseta. Past Tardajos and Rabé, the treeless plateau stretches out before dropping into the small village of Hornillos.

Did you know?

Burgos Cathedral holds the tomb of the Cid, the legendary 11th-century Castilian warlord, and his wife Jimena, placed beneath the transept in 1921.
