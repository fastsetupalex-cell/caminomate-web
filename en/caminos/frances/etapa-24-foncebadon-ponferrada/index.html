Camino Francés · Stage 24 of 33Foncebadón → Ponferrada

A high-mountain day and a long descent. Past the Cruz de Ferro, the Camino Francés's highest point, you drop through El Acebo and Molinaseca, with its lovely medieval bridge, to Ponferrada and its Templar castle.

Did you know?

At the Cruz de Ferro, around 1,500 m up, tradition holds that pilgrims lay a stone brought from home at the foot of the post, a symbol of leaving burdens and sorrows behind.
