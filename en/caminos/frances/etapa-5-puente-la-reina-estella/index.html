Stage 5: Puente la Reina – Estella

A stage through Navarrese vineyards and grain fields, crossing Cirauqui and Lorca on stretches of cobbled Roman road. It passes Villatuerta before reaching Estella, a monumental town on the Ega river.

Did you know?

The 12th-century Codex Calixtinus, the first pilgrim's guide, praised Estella as a town rich in 'good bread, excellent wine, meat and fish, and every kind of blessing'.
