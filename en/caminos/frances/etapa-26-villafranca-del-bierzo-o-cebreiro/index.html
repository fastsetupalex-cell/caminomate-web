Stage 26: Villafranca del Bierzo – O Cebreiro

The queen stage, and a tough one: after the Valcarce valley comes a long climb into the Galician mountains via Las Herrerías and La Faba. You crest at O Cebreiro, with its stone pallozas and its shrine of the eucharistic miracle.

Did you know?

O Cebreiro guards the so-called Galician Holy Grail: tradition holds that the host and wine turned to flesh and blood before a villager who doubted his faith.
