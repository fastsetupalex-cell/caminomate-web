Stage 22: San Martín del Camino – Astorga

A long stage leaving the páramo across the imposing medieval bridge of Hospital de Órbigo. After Santibáñez and the Santo Toribio cross, you reach Astorga, a Roman and Maragato town.

Did you know?

On the Hospital de Órbigo bridge, in the holy year of 1434, the knight Suero de Quiñones held the famous Paso Honroso, breaking lances against all comers to free himself from a vow of love.
