Stage 10: Santo Domingo de la Calzada – Belorado

You leave La Rioja and enter the province of Burgos, walking through Grañón and rolling grain fields. Passing Redecilla del Camino, you reach Belorado, a town with an old pottery tradition.

Did you know?

In Redecilla del Camino, the first village in Burgos, the church holds a 12th-century Romanesque baptismal font regarded as one of the finest on the Camino Francés.
