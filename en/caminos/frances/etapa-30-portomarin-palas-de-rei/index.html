Camino Francés · Stage 30 of 33Portomarín → Palas de Rei

A rolling stage of constant ups and downs through inland Lugo, among eucalyptus and oak woods and small hamlets. It passes Gonzar and Ligonde before reaching Palas de Rei, in the Ulloa country.

Did you know?

Palas de Rei is named in the 12th-century Codex Calixtinus, the first pilgrim's guide, as the end of one of its stages on the way to Santiago.
