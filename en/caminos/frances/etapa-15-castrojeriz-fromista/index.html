Stage 15: Castrojeriz – Frómista

A steep climb up the Alto de Mostelares opens the day, then flat meseta leads across the Pisuerga into Palencia province, ending at Frómista, home to one of Spain's finest Romanesque churches.

Did you know?

Frómista's 11th-century San Martín de Tours is a benchmark of full Romanesque; its cornice bears over 300 carved corbels with animals, figures and everyday scenes.
