Stage 25: Ponferrada – Villafranca del Bierzo

A gentle stage through the Bierzo, its vineyards and villages like Cacabelos, following the valley. You leave Ponferrada's Templar castle and reach Villafranca, known for its church of Santiago and Door of Forgiveness.

Did you know?

At Villafranca's church of Santiago, the Door of Forgiveness let sick pilgrims unable to reach Santiago earn the jubilee indulgence during holy years.
