Stage 8: Logroño – Nájera

Leaving Logroño through La Grajera park and its vineyards, the walking is gentle across Rioja farmland. After Navarrete you reach Nájera, an old royal capital on the banks of the Najerilla river.

Did you know?

Nájera's Monastery of Santa María la Real holds the royal pantheon of the kings of Navarre and a 16th-century Gothic cloister known as the Knights' Cloister.
