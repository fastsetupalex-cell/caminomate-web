Stage 5: A Guarda – Oia

A stage of pure Atlantic coast: the path hugs the sea almost the whole way, with waves breaking on your left. It ends in Oia, before its seaside Cistercian monastery. Little shade — protect yourself from sun and wind.

Did you know?

Above A Guarda stands Monte de Santa Trega, one of the best-preserved Celtic hillforts (castros) in Galicia. Over 2,000 years ago hundreds of people lived here in round stone houses, overlooking the mouth of the Miño river and Portugal.
