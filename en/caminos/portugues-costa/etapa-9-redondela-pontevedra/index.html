Stage 9: Redondela – Pontevedra

A stage of woodlands, Albariño vineyards and the Vigo estuary. It passes through Arcade (famous for its oysters) and the historic Sampaio bridge. It ends in Pontevedra, with one of Spain's finest old towns.

Did you know?

At the Sampaio bridge in 1809, Galician troops halted Napoleon's army, a decisive episode in driving the French out of Galicia. Today Arcade is better known for its oysters, which even have their own festival in April.
