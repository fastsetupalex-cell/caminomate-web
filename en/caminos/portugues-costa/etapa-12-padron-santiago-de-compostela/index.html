Stage 12: Padrón – Santiago de Compostela

The last one! It climbs gradually towards Santiago among sanctuaries and hamlets, crosses O Milladoiro and enters the old town up to the Obradoiro Square. Set off early to reach the pilgrim's Mass at noon.

Did you know?

This route follows the path of the legend: the apostle's body arrived by sea at Padrón and was carried from there to Compostela, where the city and cathedral were founded. Entering the Obradoiro, you'll have walked, quite literally, the Apostle's final journey.
