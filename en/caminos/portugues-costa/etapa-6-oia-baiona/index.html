Stage 6: Oia – Baiona

More wild coastline up to Cabo Silleiro and its lighthouse, then a descent into Baiona, one of Galicia's most historic towns. An exposed, windy stretch; carry water as there are few services until the end.

Did you know?

In the Serra da Groba, above Oia, wild horses have roamed free for centuries. Every summer the 'rapa das bestas' takes place: locals round them up in stone pens ('curros') to trim their manes, an ancestral festival.
