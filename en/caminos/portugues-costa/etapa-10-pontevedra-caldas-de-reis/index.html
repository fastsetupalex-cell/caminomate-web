Stage 10: Pontevedra – Caldas de Reis

Through hamlets, oak woods and stone crosses to Caldas de Reis, a spa town where you can dip your feet in naturally hot water. From here the Camino gets busier: more and more pilgrims.

Did you know?

Caldas de Reis has been a spa town since Roman times. In the centre, next to the bridge, a hot-water fountain (As Burgas) lets pilgrims soak their feet to ease tiredness. It comes out naturally at around 40 ºC!
