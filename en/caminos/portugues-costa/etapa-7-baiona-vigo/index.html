Camino Portugués de la Costa · Stage 7 of 12Baiona → Vigo

The longest stage. It leaves Baiona over the medieval bridge of A Ramallosa, follows beaches and enters Vigo, Galicia's largest city. If it feels long, you can sleep in Nigrán or A Ramallosa halfway.

Did you know?

Off Vigo lie the Cíes Islands, a national park. Their Rodas beach was once named 'the best beach in the world' by Britain's The Guardian. If you have a spare day in Vigo, the boat to the Cíes is well worth it.
